# Luah Partners — Business Workflow Questions

**Meeting date:** November 3, 2026  
**Status:** Draft for stakeholder discussion

## Purpose

The website screens are ready, but we need to agree on the business logics for the vaiours customer workflows. These answers will help us determine the workflows, systems and integrations required.

## 1. Service Requests

**Current gap:** The website describes Luah Partners' services, but the complete process for requesting and receiving each service has not been defined.

**Main question:** What should happen from the time a customer requests each service until that service is completed?

**We need to confirm: - `Customer Request Flow`**

- What information and documents are required for each service?
- Who reviews and approves each request? - are there existing portal or are we building a custom administrative portal to handle this?
- Are quotations, agreements, signatures or payments required?
- What updates should the customer receive during the process?

## 2. Listings and Leasing

**Current gap:** The Listings page currently collects a customer's property preference, but it does not provide the full journey from finding a property to signing a lease and moving in.

**Main question:** Should the website only notify customers about available properties, or should it also manage viewing, application, screening, approval, lease signing and move-in?

**We need to confirm:**

- How does a customer apply for a specific property?
- Is a viewing required before applying?
- What information, documents, consent and fees are required?
- Who reviews the application and communicates the decision?
- How will leases be prepared and signed?
- When will the deposit and first rent be paid?

## 3. Customer Account or Portal

**Current gap:** Customers currently have no place to see their service requests, progress, documents, agreements or payments after submitting a form.

**Main question:** Do customers need a Luah Partners account or portal, or will email, secure links and external provider portals be used?

**We need to confirm:**

- Which services should appear in a customer's account?
- What progress and history should customers see?
- Should customers upload documents and sign agreements there?
- Should customers see balances, payments and receipts there?
- Should customers communicate with Luah Partners through the portal?
- Can existing external systems provide any of these functions?

## 4. Service Fees, Invoicing and Payments

**Current gap:** The website mentions fees and how customers are billed, but these charges have not been translated into billing rules for each service. The Make a Payment page also does not clearly connect a payment to an identified customer, requested service or invoice.

Additionaly since the system would allow customer to make payments; what happens when something goes wrong. How does the customer reach out for possible problems due do payment failure or any other services failures? do we add a ticketing system or do we expand the current feedback form to handle these situations?.

**Main question:** For each service, what will the customer be charged, when will an invoice be generated, and how will that invoice be connected to the customer and service before payment?

**We need to confirm:**

- What fees and billing method apply to each service?
- Are charges one-off, recurring, deposit-based or paid in stages?
- What stage of the service triggers an invoice?
- Who confirms the charge and generates the invoice?
- Must the customer approve a quotation or agreement before being invoiced?
- How will each invoice be linked to the customer and requested service?
- Should customers pay only from an issued invoice instead of entering an amount themselves?
- Can someone else pay on behalf of a customer?
- Which system will manage invoices, balances, payments, receipts and refunds?

## 5. Listings Page UI and Asset Display

**Current gap:** The Listings page contains mostly explanatory text and a notification form. It does not visually present the properties or assets that customers can browse.

**Main question:** Should the Listings page be redesigned as an asset-listing experience where customers can browse available properties and open a detailed page for each one?

**We need to confirm:**

- What types of properties or assets will be listed?
- What information should appear on each listing card, such as images, location, price, bedrooms and availability?
- Does each asset need its own details page and enquiry or application action?
- Should customers be able to search, filter and sort the listings?
- Should unavailable or already leased properties remain visible?
- Where will listing information and images be managed and updated?
- What should the page show when no assets are currently available?
